Built for businesses that run on calls

Same system. Different shop floor.

We tune your agent to your exact workflow — services, providers, edge cases, the way your front desk actually talks. Four of the most common deployments we ship, and what each one handles out of the box.

Legal & real estate

Law firms & real estate agencies

Qualifies every new caller with the polish of a front-desk pro — captures the right details, screens for fit, and books consults or showings with the right person on your team.

  • New matter & new lead intake — practice area, property, timeline, budget
  • Conflict checks for firms, buyer/seller qualifying for agencies
  • Routes by specialty — practice area or listing agent / territory
  • Knows its limits — never gives legal advice or quotes a price, escalates to a human
Med spa & dental

Med spa, dental & clinics

Routes the right call to the right provider — and knows when to stop talking and put a human on the line.

  • Provider routing — injectables to the MD, facials to estheticians
  • New patient intake — DOB, insurance, primary concern
  • Pre-visit prompts (no caffeine, no retinol, arrival window)
  • HIPAA-aware transcripts, escalates clinical questions
Salons, barbershops & nail studios

Barbershops, salons & nail studios

Books across multiple chairs, stylists, and technicians — knows who does what, how long each service really takes, and how to upsell the add-ons.

  • Provider-specific booking (Mike vs. Sam vs. next available)
  • Multi-service combos — cut + beard, gel mani + nail art, etc.
  • Walk-in vs. appointment triage + waitlist text-back when a slot opens
  • SMS confirmations + 24-hour reminders to cut no-shows
Home & field services

HVAC, electricians & trades

Captures every detail a dispatcher would — address, scope, urgency — and books the right crew with travel time baked in.

  • Address + scope capture (sqft, bed/bath count, system type)
  • Crew assignment with 30-minute travel buffers
  • Emergency vs. routine call triage
  • Quote ranges from your pricebook, hands off to ops on close

FAQ

Real questions. Straight answers.

Everything owners ask on the intro call — answered up front. Still have something we missed? Pick it up on the 15-minute discovery call.

01 How much does it cost?

Every business is different. After a 30-minute discovery call we quote based on call volume, integrations, and languages. Most teams are live in about two weeks, cancel anytime.

02 Can I keep my existing phone number?

Yes. We either port your number over or set up forwarding from your current line. Your customers dial the same number they always have — nothing changes on their end.

03 What does it connect to?

We integrate with the tools you're already using — Google, Outlook, Calendly, and Acuity for scheduling; HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, GoHighLevel, and Zoho for your CRM. We also pull in your FAQs, pricing info, and business policies so the AI answers like someone who actually knows your business.

04 How long until I'm live?

Most businesses are up and running within two weeks. We handle the build, training, and testing — you just review and approve before anything goes live.

05 Do I need any technical knowledge to set this up?

None. We configure everything on our end based on a simple onboarding form and your intro call. You don't touch any code.

06 What happens when a call is too complex for the AI?

You set the rules before we go live — urgent calls, VIP clients, billing issues, after-hours situations. When something falls outside what the AI handles, it warm-transfers with context, takes a message, or schedules a callback. Nothing gets dropped.

07 What if my business has seasonal call spikes?

The AI scales automatically. Whether you get 10 calls a day or 200, it handles the volume without you adding staff or paying for idle hours.

08 Who owns my call data?

You do. Transcripts and call logs stay in your systems. We don't sell or share your data. For medical and dental practices, we offer HIPAA-compliant workflows — just bring it up on your intro call.

09 What happens when I call the demo number?

You'll reach a live AI receptionist — the same experience your customers would get. Ask it about hours, services, or booking. It takes about two minutes and requires no signup. The number is right on our site.
